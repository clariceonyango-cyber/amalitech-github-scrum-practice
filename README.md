## Scrum Framework

Scrum is a lightweight framework that helps teams create value through adaptive solutions for complex problems.

### Scrum Accountabilities

- Product Owner: Maximises the value of the product.
- Scrum Master: Helps the team understand and use Scrum effectively.
- Developers: Create a usable Increment during each Sprint.

### Scrum Events

- Sprint: A fixed-length period for achieving the Sprint Goal.
- Sprint Planning: The team plans the work for the Sprint.
- Daily Scrum: Developers inspect progress towards the Sprint Goal.
- Sprint Review: The team inspects the outcome of the Sprint.
- Sprint Retrospective: The team identifies ways to improve.

### Scrum Artifacts

- Product Backlog: An ordered list of work needed for the product.
- Sprint Backlog: The Sprint Goal, selected Product Backlog items, and plan for delivering them.
- Increment: A usable step towards the product goal.

### Scrum Commitments

- Product Goal: The long-term objective for the product.
- Sprint Goal: The single objective for the Sprint.
- Definition of Done: The formal description of the quality requirements for an Increment.
